Personal information we collect

We collect personal information from you directly, e.g. when you play games or register for an account, and also generate information about your use of our games, including game play and log data. If you log in to a game with your social media account, we will receive information about you including your name, profile picture and email address. How we use your personal information

We use your information to provide you with our games, support and analyse your use, and improve them. We will also use your information to respond to your questions. We sometimes send you promotional emails, send you push notifications and show you ads targeted to you in our games. You can choose to stop receiving these at any time, by opting out or changing your browser or device settings.

We rely on third parties for the purposes set out in the privacy policy, including support services, such as game servers, support ticketing and game analytics. We also work with certain advertising partners to show you ads. Additionally, we share your information within our group and as required by law.

Where we process your personal information

We have locations in the United States, Switzerland and the EEA where we process your information. Some of our providers are located outside of the EEA such as the United States and Israel.

Your rights

You may have rights to access, receive a copy of, or delete, your information or restrict processing of your data, and you also have the right to object to our use of your information for certain purposes, including tailored marketing and advertising.

Changes to the Privacy Policy

Any updates or changes to this Policy will be published here. If there are any significant changes, we will notify you through the website and in-Game.

Introduction

This privacy policy ("Policy") informs you of your choices and our practices in relation to your Information (as defined below). BOBCAT TECHNOLOGY CO., LIMITED, operating under the developer name Bad Luck Inc, is the data controller under the applicable privacy laws for the games published under the name Bad Luck Inc on Google Play.

For the purpose of this Policy, "Information" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable individual.

1. PERSONAL INFORMATION WE COLLECT

We will collect and use the following Information about you:

Information you provide to us

Registration information: for example, if you register to play a game on the Website, you will be asked to provide your date of birth, email address and a password, and to create an avatar.

Information collected via our customer support channels: for example, when you interact with us via Zendesk, our customer support provider, we ask you to provide (a) your full name, email and password, and (b) any information you choose to provide to allow us to assist you.

Information you provide when using the Games: when you play the Games, we may ask you to choose a username. Also, if you choose to invite your friends to play a game on the Website, we will collect the email address of your friends.

Information collected through surveys: we may ask you to complete surveys about your experience.

Information we automatically collect or is generated about you when you play the Games

Identifiers, such as your IP address, device and app ID, unique ID, city level location data and device information (such as model, brand and operating system).

Cookies: we use cookies and other similar technologies to enhance your experience.

Information you generate and display when playing the Games, such as profile photos and chat content.

Information regarding your use of the Games, such as date and time stamps of game events, interactions with our teams and ad engagement.

Information received from third parties

Information we receive from third party platforms when you register: Apple, Facebook or Google Play.

Information from payment processors: Apple, Google and Xsolla.

Analytics information: Mobius Solutions Ltd (Optimove).

Information collected from advertising partners: Unity Technologies.

2. HOW WE USE YOUR PERSONAL INFORMATION

We use your Information to:

Provide you with the Games: Including account creation, progress saving, and in-game purchases.

Improve and monitor Game use: To analyze performance and technical stability.

Provide support: To respond to your requests or complaints.

Conduct analytics: To create anonymised and aggregated data.

Marketing & Advertising: To send updates about in-Game offers and provide tailored advertising where permitted.

Security & Fraud Prevention: To prevent abuse and comply with legal obligations.

3. WHO WE SHARE YOUR PERSONAL INFORMATION WITH

We share your Information with:

Other players (profile and chat data).

Vendors and service providers: Amazon Web Services (Storage), Zendesk (Support), Adjust GmbH, Optimove, Flurry (Analytics).

Advertising partners: Including AdColony, Google AdMob, Unity Ads, etc.

Payment processors: Apple, Google, Xsolla.

Group Companies: To operate the Games efficiently.

Law enforcement agencies when legally required.

4. WHERE WE PROCESS YOUR PERSONAL INFORMATION

Your Information will be processed by BOBCAT TECHNOLOGY CO., LIMITED's employees and service providers in the EEA, Switzerland, Israel, USA, and South Korea, with appropriate safeguards (Standard Contractual Clauses) in place.

Your Information is kept for as long as you are registered or using our games, and then for up to three years from the date you stop interacting with the games.
